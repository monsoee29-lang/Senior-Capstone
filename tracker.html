📐 How Your CO2 Footprint is Calculated

Each plastic item has an estimated weight and a life-cycle emission factor. Your weekly plastic mass and CO2e are calculated using these values:

# Plastic Item Weight per Unit (g) Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/kg)
1Plastic bags75.4

6Food sachets25.6
Step 1 — Total Plastic Mass:
Total Plastic (g) = ∑ (Number of items × Weight per unit)

Example:
• Plastic bags: 3 × 7 g = 21 g (0.021 kg)
• Bottled water: 1 × 10 g = 10 g (0.010 kg)
Total Daily Consumption: 21 g + 10 g = 31 g (0.031 kg)
Step 2 — CO₂e Emissions:
Estimated CO₂e (kg) = ∑ (Total plastic used per type (kg) × Specific Emission Factor)

Example:
• Plastic bags: 0.021 kg × 5.4 = 0.1134 kg CO₂e/day
• Bottled water: 0.010 kg × 5.9 = 0.0590 kg CO₂e/day
Total Estimated Daily CO₂e: 0.1134 + 0.0590 = 0.1724 kg CO₂e
